The classic 4th-power area-ratio law is a strong idealization (planar, collisionless sheaths, no parasitic capacitance). Real self-bias also depends on sheath capacitance ratio, chamber geometry, and process conditions — use this for intuition, not for predicting an exact tool's DC bias reading.

ABOUT THE IEDF
Real ions arrive with a distribution of energies (the Ion Energy Distribution Function, IEDF), not a single sharp value. At low RF frequency relative to the ion transit time, the IEDF is broad (bimodal); at high frequency, ions respond only to the time-averaged sheath field and the IEDF narrows toward this estimate.

E_ion [eV] ≈ Z · V_sheath [V]
