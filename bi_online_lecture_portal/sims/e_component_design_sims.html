INSIGHT

These replace the unverified "Cshunt = 1.152 x Coss" figure from the earlier infographic draft. The real relationships (Sokal / Raab): shunt susceptance omega x C1 = 0.1836/R, and optimum series reactance is about 1.15 x R, split between L0 and C0 once you pick a loaded Q. Higher Q means better harmonic filtering but bulkier, lossier components -- the classic RF tradeoff. There is no fixed Cshunt/Coss ratio; the device's own Coss just needs to be less than the required C1, with any shortfall added externally.

Derived the same way as the drain-voltage waveform, from the node current balance Idc = i_switch + i_tank while ON. It lines up with the commonly cited approximation of about 1.7 x Vdd/R for peak switch current. This is the number that actually stresses the DE375-102N12A (12A continuous rating) -- worth checking against the datasheet's pulsed/RF current rating, not just the DC figure.
