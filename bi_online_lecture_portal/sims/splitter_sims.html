What the Poynting Vector Mode Is Showing You

Switch to Poynting Vector mode and look closely: the purple arrows travel in the space around the conductor, not through it. That space is where RF power actually flows — the conductor itself mainly guides the electromagnetic field. This is the same physics (S = E × H) behind every transmission line, splitter, and matching network you\u2019ll work with.
