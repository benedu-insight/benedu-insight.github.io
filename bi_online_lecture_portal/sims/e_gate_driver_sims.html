INSIGHT

A perfectly centered threshold (0%) gives a clean 50/50 duty cycle — exactly what push-pull switching needs. Any DC offset or bias drift in the comparator shifts the threshold off-center and skews the duty cycle, which is why threshold precision matters for ZVS timing downstream.
