INSIGHT

A properly matched plasma process (VSWR around 1.05) puts reflected power more than 30dB below forward -- deep inside the AD8310's verified 90dB range, but pushing against a typical diode detector's practical floor (noise, offset, and the thermistor's imperfect temperature compensation all matter more as the signal gets small). This is exactly why the number that gets reported to the host comes from the heterodyne channel, not the fast diode channel that protects against arcs.
