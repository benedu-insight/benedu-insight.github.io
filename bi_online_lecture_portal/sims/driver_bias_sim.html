NOTE

Why Single-Ended Driver Stages Often Run Class B

A single device can only conduct current for part of the cycle once biased below Class A — a push-pull pair would normally split the two halves between two devices, but a single-ended stage has no partner to hand off to. RF circuits tolerate this far better than audio does, since the resonant tank shown in Stage 2 below reconstructs the missing half of the cycle on its own. That's what lets a single-ended driver bias right at V_th — Class B — without leaving a gap in the output waveform.
