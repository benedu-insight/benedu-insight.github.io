2.5D PACKAGING & SYSTEM OVERVIEW
Silicon Interposer acts as a dense routing substrate to enable high-density connections with very small spacing between GPU and HBM, bypassing traditional PCB limitations. Continuous vertical copper channels (TSVs) connect DRAM stacks.
