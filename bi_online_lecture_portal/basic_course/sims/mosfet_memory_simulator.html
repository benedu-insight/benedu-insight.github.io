Why Does DRAM Need Refresh?
Capacitor leakage current gradually destroys stored data
LEAKAGE
The storage capacitor (~10–30 fF) slowly loses charge through MOSFET sub-threshold leakage. Without refresh, a stored “1” decays to “0” within ~64 ms — making DRAM volatile memory.
